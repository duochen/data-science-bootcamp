**Homework Assignment: Data Science Research Project & FigShare Publication**

---

### **Objective**

Apply all the skills learned in the Data Science Bootcamp to complete a full data science project — from data collection to publication. The goal is to conduct a meaningful research project using a public dataset, analyze it with machine learning techniques, write a structured research paper, and publish it on FigShare to obtain a DOI.

---

### **Deliverables**

1. **Jupyter Notebook (.ipynb)** – Includes data loading, cleaning, visualization, ML model training, and evaluation.
2. **Research Paper (PDF)** – 3–5 pages written in a professional, scientific format.
3. **FigShare Publication** – Upload the paper and files to FigShare and obtain a DOI.
4. **Presentation Slides (Optional)** – 5–10 slides summarizing the project.

---

### **Step-by-Step Instructions**

#### **Step 1: Choose a Topic and Dataset**

* Explore public dataset sources (Kaggle, UCI, Data.gov, Google Dataset Search).
* Pick a topic that interests you: health, environment, finance, education, or social media.
* Download and explore the dataset (at least 500 rows recommended).

#### **Step 2: Data Cleaning and Preprocessing**

* Handle missing data (`dropna`, `fillna`, or imputation methods).
* Encode categorical variables and normalize numeric features.
* Select relevant features and split data into train/test sets.

#### **Step 3: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

* Visualize distributions (histograms, box plots).
* Explore relationships (pair plots, correlation heatmaps).
* Write short observations for each finding.

#### **Step 4: Apply Machine Learning**

* Choose one type of model: **Regression** (predict numbers) or **Classification** (predict categories).
* Example algorithms: Linear Regression, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, SVM.
* Evaluate with proper metrics: RMSE/R² for regression or Accuracy/F1/ROC-AUC for classification.

#### **Step 5: Summarize Your Results**

* Include performance metrics in a table.
* Use charts (confusion matrix, residual plots, etc.) to visualize results.
* Interpret findings: What do they mean? What are limitations?

#### **Step 6: Write Your Research Paper**

**Format:**

1. **Title** – Concise and descriptive.
2. **Abstract (150–250 words)** – Summarize dataset, methods, and findings.
3. **Introduction** – Explain motivation and research question.
4. **Methods** – Describe preprocessing and ML model.
5. **Results** – Present outcomes, graphs, and evaluation.
6. **Discussion & Conclusion** – Interpret insights and future work.
7. **References** – Cite datasets, libraries, and tools used.

#### **Step 7: Publish on FigShare**

1. Go to [https://figshare.com](https://figshare.com) and create a free account.
2. Upload your paper (PDF), dataset, and code notebook.
3. Add title, authors, description, and keywords.
4. Choose license (CC BY recommended).
5. Click **Publish** to obtain your DOI.

#### **Step 8: Submit Your Work**

Submit the following items:

* FigShare DOI link.
* Jupyter Notebook (.ipynb) file.
* Research Paper (PDF).
* Optional: 5–10 slide summary of your project.
